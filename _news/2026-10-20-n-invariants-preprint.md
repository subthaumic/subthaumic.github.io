New preprint: **Distributions of metric *n*-point invariants and applications to molecular evolution** with Mario Gómez, Facundo Mémoli, Maximilian Neumann and Andreas Ott is now on [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26070). We show how many sampled *n*-point configurations it takes to approximate the *n*-point geometry of a space, and use the resulting distributions to study a large data set of SARS-CoV-2 genomes. Code: [n-invariants](https://github.com/subthaumic/n-invariants).
