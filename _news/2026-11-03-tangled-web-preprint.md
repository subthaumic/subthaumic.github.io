New preprint: **The Tangled Web They Weave: Exploring Neural Representations with Directed Simplicial Complexes** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.00786) (accepted at NeurReps 2026). Counterfactual ablations of groups of neurons yield filtered directed simplicial complexes that capture the higher-order causal dependencies behind distributed feature representations. I've previously written about this here [blog post](https://open.substack.com/pub/subthaumic/p/the-tangled-web-they-weave).
